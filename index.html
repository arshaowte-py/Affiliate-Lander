Questions.

Who can apply?

Creators on Instagram, YouTube, Facebook or X whose audience trusts what they recommend. Home, fitness, parenting, WFH, health and review creators tend to fit Frido best.

Do I need a big following?

No. How consistently you publish matters more than how many followers you have.

How much can I earn?

You earn commission on every attributed order. Your rate is shared with you when you're approved, and it grows as you move up. What you earn depends on your content and your audience, and no income is guaranteed.

When is commission confirmed and paid?

Commission is confirmed once an order is delivered and not returned, then paid to your registered account on a regular payout cycle. You can follow every order and payout in your creator wallet.

Will I get products to try?

Depending on your creator profile, audience and tier, you may unlock opportunities to experience Frido products before you create with them.

I'm already a Frido affiliate. Should I apply?

Yes. Applying here moves you into the new creator programme, with your existing code noted on your profile.

What happens after I apply?

We review every application. Approved creators get their code, their link, a first brief and an invite to the Comfort Club.

Does it cost anything to join?

No. Joining the Frido creator programme is free.
